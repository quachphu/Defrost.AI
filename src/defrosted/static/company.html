01 — Mission

Renting and buying are broken. We're fixing the side no one fights for.

Finding a home in America means weeks of searching, dozens of unanswered messages, stacked application fees, and a market where every major platform makes its money from the other side of the table. Renters and buyers are the users — but they've never been the customer.

Defrost.AI gives every renter and buyer what wealthy clients have always had: a tireless, expert agent in their corner. Ours is an AI — it reads the market in real time, writes to landlords and sellers the moment a listing appears, negotiates the terms you approve, and prepares legally sound paperwork. Always on, never commissioned, structurally on your side.

"Every incumbent optimizes for the landlord's outcome. We exist to optimize for yours."

    04 — Trust & compliance

    A housing company has to earn trust the boring way.

    Real estate is one of the most regulated consumer categories in the country — and it should be. Here's how we operate, in plain language. Expand any item.

    Soliciting rentals and negotiating lease terms for compensation is licensed brokerage activity in most states — including California, our first market. We're built to operate under a licensed California DRE broker structure from day one, with our agent's negotiation activity supervised the same way a human agent's would be.
    • Brokerage-supervised negotiation in every licensed state we serve
    • State-by-state licensing buildout as we expand — the moat is doing this right
    • License and broker-of-record details published as we launch each state
    The Fair Housing Act applies to algorithms, and we build like it. Our matching never uses race, color, religion, sex, familial status, national origin, or disability — or proxies for them — and we follow HUD's 2024 guidance on AI in housing.
    • Documented, audited filtering logic — no demographic proxies, no steering
    • Every agent conversation logged and reviewable
    • Ongoing monitoring for disparate outcomes, not just intent
    Our agent contacts landlords and sellers in writing — through listing reply forms where they've invited inquiries, and compliant email. That's the whole channel list: no robocalls, no AI voice calls, ever. It's better for trust and it's the right side of the law.
    • Email that follows CAN-SPAM: honest subject lines, our real identity and address, opt-outs honored
    • Our agent always discloses that it's an AI (California SB 1001 bot-disclosure compliant)
    • Opt out once and we never write again
    Our listings come from licensed data feeds and official APIs — not from scraping platforms against their terms. Your personal data is handled under CCPA/CPRA: we tell you what we collect, you can see it, and you can delete it.
    • Licensed listing feeds and syndication partners only
    • CCPA/CPRA rights honored — access, deletion, do-not-share
    • Financial details used only to qualify you, never sold
    If our AI says it, we own it. Courts have made clear a company is responsible for its bot's representations — we agree, and we build for it. Nothing binding happens without your explicit approval, and a human reviews every commitment before it's made.
    • Human-in-the-loop approval before any offer, application, or signature
    • Full audit log of every message our agent sends on your behalf
    • Accuracy controls on availability, pricing, and lease terms
    Deposits and rent flow directly from you to the landlord through licensed payment processors. Defrost.AI never takes custody of your funds — no commingling, no float, no surprises.
    • Payments move renter → landlord directly, via licensed processors
    • No commissions taken from either side of your deal
    • Application fees are the landlord's, capped and itemized where state law requires
    05 — Principles

    Four rules we don't break.

    01
    The renter is the customer
    Not the product, not the lead. Every incentive in the company points at your outcome: better home, better terms, faster.
    02
    In writing, with consent
    Our agent works in written channels where contact was invited, identifies itself as an AI, and honors every opt-out. No calls — period.
    03
    Nothing binding without you
    The agent researches, drafts, and negotiates. You approve. Every offer, application, and signature is explicitly yours.
    04
    Free means free
    No commission on either side of the deal. When we charge for premium capability, the price is on the label — never hidden in your rent.
