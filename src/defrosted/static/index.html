Legal protections
Your agent always has your back

Whether you're a renter, buyer, landlord, or seller — every deal on Defrost.AI includes legally sound terms to protect both parties.

🛡️
For Renters
  • Deposit returned within 21 days of move-out, itemized in writing
  • 5-day grace period before any late fees apply
  • Landlord must give 24-hour notice before entry (non-emergency)
  • Habitable condition guaranteed — issues addressed within 72 hours
  • No retaliation evictions for good-faith complaints
🔒
For Landlords & Sellers
  • Security deposit (1–2 months rent) held in escrow before keys transfer
  • Earnest money (1–3% of sale price) non-refundable if buyer defaults
  • Income verification: tenants must earn ≥ 3× monthly rent
  • Lease-break penalty: 2 months rent unless military or DV exemption
  • Unlawful detainer filing if tenant refuses to vacate after notice
📜
For Buyers (Purchase)
  • 10-day inspection contingency — cancel or negotiate repairs
  • 21-day financing contingency — loan falls through? Get your deposit back
  • Appraisal contingency — property must appraise at or above offer price
  • Title insurance required — protects against undisclosed liens
  • Closing in 30–45 days with complete title transfer
