How it works

One conversation. Then your agent works.

You describe what you need — budget, area, move-in date, must-haves. From there, your AI agent runs the search the way a good human agent would: methodically, in writing, and never without your sign-off.

The pipeline

Six steps, start to keys.

1
Profile
Tell your agent your city, budget, income, credit range, move-in date, and requirements — once. It builds the complete picture a leasing office would ask for, so you never re-type it.
2
Analysis
Your agent runs a rent-vs-buy analysis on your numbers and tells you what you can actually afford — before you fall in love with anything.
3
Discovery
It watches live listings that match your criteria and surfaces the ones worth your time, with the trade-offs spelled out. No discriminatory filtering, ever — matching uses your stated housing criteria only.
4
Written outreach — with your authorization
For listings you pick, your agent contacts the landlord or property manager in writing only — email and listing reply forms. It identifies itself as an AI agent acting on your behalf, and nothing is sent until you authorize it. It tracks replies and follows up so you don't have to chase anyone.
5
Negotiation drafts — you approve
When there's something to negotiate — rent, move-in date, lease terms — your agent prepares the message and shows it to you first. You approve, edit, or reject. It never commits you to anything.
6
Tour & lease coordination
Your agent helps schedule tours, keeps every thread organized, and walks you through the lease step by step so nothing gets signed in a hurry.
The consent moment

Nothing goes out without you.

Before your agent contacts anyone, you see exactly what it will send, to whom, and why — and you say yes or no. That authorization is explicit, logged, and revocable at any time.

Hard limits

What your agent will never do.

Never calls anyone

No phone calls, no voicemail, no texting. Outreach is written-only — email and listing reply forms — full stop.

Never commits you

It can't accept terms, sign anything, or promise money on your behalf. Every commitment requires your explicit confirmation.

Never misstates the facts

It sends what you approved — nothing more. And we stand behind what our agent says: if it's wrong, that's on us, not on you.
