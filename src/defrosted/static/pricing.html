Who pays, and why it matters

Listing sites earn advertising and lead fees from the properties they show you, so their real customer is the landlord. Defrost.AI takes no money from landlords or property managers — no placement fees, no promoted listings, no lead sales. When your agent recommends a place, there's no one paying us to make it rank higher.
