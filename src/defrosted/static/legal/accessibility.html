Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 2026

Our commitment

Housing search should work for everyone. Defrost.AI targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance across the site and product, and we treat accessibility bugs as real bugs.

What we've done so far

  • Semantic landmarks, skip-to-content link, and keyboard-reachable navigation (including the mobile menu).
  • Visible focus states; forms with labels and inline error messages.
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected — animations are disabled for users who ask.
  • Alt text on meaningful images; decorative graphics hidden from screen readers.

Honest current state

We are early, and parts of the site predate this statement. We are auditing legacy pages (including our interactive app) against WCAG 2.1 AA and fixing gaps as we find them. [[PLACEHOLDER: date of first full audit when completed]]

Feedback & accommodations

If you hit a barrier, or need help using Defrost.AI in an accessible way, contact us and we'll work with you directly: [[PLACEHOLDER: accessibility@ email]] or /contact. Please include the page and what went wrong; we aim to respond within two business days.
