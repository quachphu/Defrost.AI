Our commitment

Defrost.AI supports and complies with the federal Fair Housing Act and California's fair-housing laws. Housing must be available to everyone regardless of race, color, religion, sex (including gender identity and sexual orientation), national origin, familial status, or disability — and California law protects additional characteristics, including source of income. An AI agent doesn't get an exemption: under HUD's 2024 guidance, using an algorithm doesn't move responsibility; we hold it.

How an automated agent avoids discriminatory filtering

  • Tenancy-relevant criteria only. Matching and outreach use your budget, desired size, locations, move-in timing, and stated requirements — never protected characteristics, and the system does not attempt to infer them.
  • No proxy criteria. We review matching logic for features that could stand in for protected classes (and we refuse user instructions that would — e.g., filtering by the perceived demographics of a building or block).
  • Steering is a failure mode, not a feature. The agent presents what matches your stated criteria; it does not nudge you toward or away from neighborhoods on any basis other than those criteria.
  • Logged and auditable. Matching and outreach decisions are logged so that behavior can be audited. [[PLACEHOLDER: describe audit cadence once established]]

If something looks wrong

If you believe Defrost.AI produced a discriminatory outcome, tell us and we will investigate: [[PLACEHOLDER: fairhousing@ email]] or /contact. You can also file a complaint directly with HUD's Office of Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity or the California Civil Rights Department.
